# EX200 practice

From 2026-10-09 to 2026-10-24 I practised for the RHCSA exam (EX200). A practice exam is like the real one: 3 hours, two fresh VMs, 300 points over the 10 exam areas, 210 to pass, and everything is checked after a reboot, so a setting that does not survive it counts as not done. My bar: three exams in a row with at least 255/300, in 2:30 or less, and no area under 70 %. Exams 13, 15, 16 and 19 met it, but never three in a row.

Guided exams 01–04 let me ask for help. From 2026-10-14 every exam was solo, with no hints, no notes and the same start state as the real one, and the drills were for learning, with questions allowed. From exam 07 I used the VM console only where the real exam needs it. After exam 17 I stopped the full exams, because every solo exam from 09 on was a pass, and did drills on topics I picked.
