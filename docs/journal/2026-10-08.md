# 2026-10-08: Storage drill on db2, GRUB and boot targets

Two things on db2: a drill for the RHCSA storage objectives (ext4 grow and shrink, swap on an LV, a VG that is really full), then break and fix with GRUB and boot targets. The scary moment: I put the LVM PV UUID of the system disk into fstab. `mount -a` caught it. After a reboot, db2 would have stopped in emergency mode.

Phase 1 · Docs: [VMs](../architecture/vms.md) · Checks: [07](../../checks/07-postgres-remote.sh), [08](../../checks/08-tuned-dba.sh), [10](../../checks/10-nfs.sh)

## What I did
- Ran the drill inside a budget of 2044 MiB in VG `data`, without touching LV `backup`.
- LV `scratch` (1 GiB), ext4, mounted by `LABEL=scratch`, with a 200 MiB test file and its checksum.
- Grew `scratch` to 1.5 GiB online and shrank it back to 1 GiB with `lvreduce -r`. The checksum still matched.
- LV `swaptest` (512 MiB) as swap, in fstab by UUID, next to `rhel/swap`.
- Filled a VG on a temporary 1 GiB disk, saw `lvextend` fail, and fixed it with a second disk and `vgextend`.
- Rebooted db2 like an exam grader would: both mounts, both swaps and `systemctl --failed` were fine.
- Cleaned up in reverse order, down to `virsh vol-delete` on the NUC. After another reboot, fstab had its 5 lines again.
- Booted db2 once into `emergency.target` from GRUB. With root locked, it gave me no shell.
- Switched the default target with `systemctl set-default` and changed kernel arguments with `grubby --update-kernel=ALL`.
- Covered `rd.break` and a bad fstab line in theory only, to drill in phase 2 on a VM with a graphical console.
- Put db2 back: only `console=ttyS0,115200` on the kernel line, no VNC, and check 10 passed.

## What broke
- **`mount -a` failed with `/dev/vda3: Can't open blockdev`.** I had forgotten `mkfs.xfs` and copied the PV UUID of the system disk from `blkid` without sudo. `sudo blkid -s UUID -o value /dev/exam/docs` gave the right one.
- **`df -h /mnt/docs` showed `/dev/mapper/rhel-root`.** The directory was not a mount point yet, so files written there would land on root and be hidden by the later mount.
- **The serial console lost its login prompt.** `grubby --args="console=tty0"` replaced `console=ttyS0,115200`, because grubby first removes every argument with the same name. Both go in one call.
- **GRUB over the serial console (`virsh console`) was unusable**: a garbled screen and keys in the wrong place. The VNC console worked.
