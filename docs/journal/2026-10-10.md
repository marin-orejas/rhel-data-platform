## What broke and how I fixed it
- The nice drop-in failed again, this time with `[service]` in lower case. Section and key names in unit files are case-sensitive. A read-back with `systemctl show -p Nice <unit>` and `ps -o ni` would have caught it.
- I put the sshd `Port` lines in `/etc/ssh/ssh_config.d/`, the SSH client's directory. The server reads `/etc/ssh/sshd_config.d/`, and `sshd -T | grep ^port` shows what it really uses.
- One space after a comma in an fstab options field (`defaults, ro`) split the line: `ro` landed in the dump field, and the share was mounted read-write. `findmnt --verify` reports it as a parse error.
- A typo in an IPv6 prefix put the address in another /64, so the other host was off-link (`Network is unreachable`). `ping -6` right after the change would have shown it.
- I changed `GRUB_TIMEOUT` in `/etc/default/grub` but never ran `grub2-mkconfig -o /boot/grub2/grub.cfg`, so the boot menu kept the old value.
