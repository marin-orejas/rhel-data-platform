## What broke and how I fixed it
- RHEL 10.2 has no `/etc/systemd/journald.conf`. A persistent journal goes in a drop-in, `/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/*.conf` with `[Journal]` and `Storage=persistent`, or comes from creating `/var/log/journal`.
- `usermod -u` changes the owner of the files in the home directory only. Files elsewhere keep the old UID, and `find / -uid <old>` finds them.
- `useradd -M` makes no home directory.
- A service drop-in with `RestartSec=` but no `Restart=` does nothing: without `Restart=` systemd never restarts the service.
- I added the same `at` job twice. `atq` before and after shows it.
