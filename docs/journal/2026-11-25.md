# 2026-11-25: An etcd restore and a Kubernetes upgrade

A restore from an etcd snapshot brought back a deleted namespace with the API down for 45.9 s, and a timer on k8s1 now takes a snapshot every night. Then kubeadm took all three nodes from 1.36.6 to 1.36.7, and each drain of the primary's node stopped writes for about 20 s, with 0 rows lost. On the way the old primary `pg-2` sat in `Terminating` for 30 minutes, and k8s2 came back from its first pass still on 1.36.6.

## What I did
- Installed `etcdctl` and `etcdutl` 3.6.8 on k8s1. A snapshot by hand: 31617056 bytes in 0.39 s, though 9.3 MB is in use.
- A timer runs `etcd-snapshot.sh` at 01:00 UTC, and the script keeps the newest 7. Eight runs by hand left exactly 7.
- The failover client takes its table from `FAILOVER_TABLE` now. I started it on `etcd_restore_test`, through `pg-rw`.
- Took a snapshot, deleted the namespace `etcd-test`, moved the four manifests out and ran `etcdutl snapshot restore`.
- The API was down 45.9 s. `etcd-test` was back, and its pods ran again about 71 s after the manifests went out.
- The kubelet rebuilt the pods alone, 19–25 s after `readyz`, with the same IPs. My restart of it changed nothing.
- The client logged 0 FAIL in 2,248 inserts and wrote on after I saved the log: 2,372 rows, none lost.
- Check 35 runs from the desktop, through Ansible and the API tunnel, and passed the first time.
- Chose 1.36.7 and CRI-O 1.36.9, not 1.37: CloudNativePG 1.30, cert-manager 1.21 and TopoLVM 0.42 list up to 1.36.
- Upgraded k8s1 first, then k8s3 and k8s2: drain, upgrade, reboot, uncordon, with the client on `upgrade_test`.
- The k8s1 drain moved both CoreDNS pods. One insert waited 5.3 s, with no FAIL.
- The k8s1 reboot took the API away for 30–61 s. The `upgrade apply` outage is lost: `tee` without `-a` overwrote its log.
- The drains of k8s3 and k8s2 each started a switchover first. Writes stopped 20.3 s and 20.1 s, with 29 and 32 FAIL.
- `upgrade_test` has 10,174 rows, none lost. The gaps 1327–1350 and 7900–7927 are the sequence jumps at the promotions.
- `kubernetes/kubeadm/config.yaml` pins `v1.36.7` now. Check 36 passed at once, with no negative test.

## What broke
- **Right after `readyz` both pods of `etcd-test` showed `Running` with `STARTED 15:52:06Z`, from before the delete.** For about 20 s the API served the pod status from the snapshot. `crictl pods` on k8s2 and k8s3 showed the new sandboxes.
- **`pg-2` stayed `Terminating` for 30 minutes, and the k8s3 drain stopped at its 600 s timeout.** The eviction came 2 s after the switchover had restarted its `postgres` container, so the kubelet waited the whole grace period, CloudNativePG's `stopDelay` of 1800 s. It was a standby by then, so I waited with `kubectl wait --for=delete` and drained again.
- **k8s2 came back from its reboot on `v1.36.6`.** `dnf.log` said `No match for argument: kubeadm-1.36.7`, because the cached metadata was from before 1.36.7 came out that day. The lines ran on anyway, locked 1.36.6 again and rebooted. A second pass with `dnf install --refresh` and the steps joined by `&&` worked.
