# 2026-09-30: Persistent journal

I wanted the logs to survive a reboot on db1, db2 and the NUC. They do now, from `/var/log/journal`. It took more than one setting: a drop-in, a flush and the permissions, and most of my mistakes gave no error at all.

Phase 1 · Docs: [Journal](../architecture/system.md#journal)

## What I did
- Made the journal persistent with a drop-in: `Storage=persistent` in the `[Journal]` section.
- Restarted `systemd-journald` and moved the logs to `/var/log/journal` with `journalctl --flush`.
- Fixed the group and ACLs on `/var/log/journal` with `systemd-tmpfiles --create --prefix /var/log/journal`.

## What broke
- **`/etc/systemd/journald.conf` does not exist on RHEL 10.** The defaults are in `/usr/lib/systemd/`, and my changes go in a drop-in under `/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/`.
- **The section is `[Journal]`, not `[Journald]`.** systemd does not stop on a wrong section name, it only logs a warning, so I check `journalctl -u systemd-journald` after a change.
- **After the restart, journald still wrote to `/run/log/journal`.** It moves to `/var/log/journal` only after `journalctl --flush`.
- **After the flush, `journalctl` without sudo showed `-- No entries --`**, with no warning. The new directory had no `systemd-journal` group and no ACL, and `systemd-tmpfiles` fixed it.
