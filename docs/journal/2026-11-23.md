# 2026-11-23: PostgreSQL on CloudNativePG and a failover

When I cut the power to the primary's worker with `virsh destroy k8s2`, CloudNativePG promoted the standby on its own. Writes stopped for 72.9 s, and none of the 658 confirmed rows were lost. By hand on db1 and db2 it took 5 min 51 s ([2026-10-29](2026-10-29.md)). I don't know yet why the promotion came 16 s after k8s2 went `NotReady`.

## What I did
- Installed the CloudNativePG operator 1.30.2 with Helm: two replicas on two workers, so one is left when a worker dies.
- Made the cluster `pg`: PostgreSQL 18.7, `pg-1` primary on k8s2, `pg-2` standby on k8s3, 512Mi and a 1Gi volume each.
- Logged in as `app` through `pg-rw` and got the primary, as `report` through `pg-ro` and got the standby.
- Ran `scripts/failover-client.sh` in a pod on k8s3 against `pg-rw`. It takes its conninfo from `FAILOVER_CONNINFO` now.
- `virsh destroy k8s2` at 20:01:56 UTC: 49 failed inserts, `pg-2` promoted after 68 s, the first new row after 72.9 s.
- Counted by the `server` column, not by id: 658 rows confirmed by `pg-1`, 658 in the table. Ids 659–683 were skipped.
- k8s2 came back after about 14 minutes. `pg-1` was made again on its volume and rejoined as the standby by `pg_rewind`.
- A `max_wal_size` patch failed exactly two checks in check 32. After a reboot of all three nodes, checks 25–32 passed.

## What broke
- **`helm show chart cnpg/cloudenative-pg` said `not found in cnpg index. (try 'helm repo update')`.** The chart name was misspelled, so `helm repo update` could not help. Helm gives the same hint for a typo and for a stale index.
- **`kubectl create configmap` made an empty ConfigMap `failover-client`, with no warning.** I had left out `--from-file`. I saw it before the pod started, deleted it and made it again.
- **The first run of check 32 failed on the row count: 2,532 expected, 2,738 in the table.** The number came from `~/failover-32.log`, saved at 20:09:44 while the client still wrote. The log holds the measurement, and the table holds the totals.
- **dnf printed `Warning: skipped OpenPGP checks for 1 package from repository: @commandline`.** The `kubectl cnpg` RPM from the release URL is signed, key ID 07bdb5f9e33e0d6d, but nothing checked it.
