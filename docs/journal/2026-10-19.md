## What broke and how I fixed it
- A script printed line numbers instead of the lines, because I used `grep -n` and then `cut`. I now compare the output with the task's wording before I move on.
- A firewall port was gone after the reboot. A port must be in the permanent config: `firewall-cmd --permanent --add-port=…` and `firewall-cmd --reload`, then `firewall-cmd --list-ports` to check.
- An autofs map pointed to the wrong export path. `showmount -e <server>` lists the real exports, and a `cd` into a key tests the mount.
- A VFAT file system has no Unix owners. The owner comes from the `uid=` and `gid=` mount options in fstab.
- `lvextend -L` rounds up to whole extents. With 32 MiB extents, my size landed outside the allowed range. `vgdisplay` shows the extent size; I pick a multiple of it, or use `-l` with extents.
