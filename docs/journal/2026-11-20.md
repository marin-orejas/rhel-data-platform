# 2026-11-20: TopoLVM volumes and a StatefulSet

I wanted volumes that live on the workers' own disks and can grow. k8s2 and k8s3 have a 5 GiB data disk each now, TopoLVM makes an LV on it for every claim, and `data-writer-0` grew from 1Gi to 2Gi in about a second while its pod kept writing, with 0 restarts. The reboot at the end took k8s1 down: at 2.5 GiB it stopped answering six and a half minutes after boot, so it has 3 GiB now.

## What I did
- Gave k8s2 and k8s3 a 5 GiB thin qcow2 each as `vdb`, with one partition and an empty VG `data`. k8s1 has none.
- A hand-made `local` PV went `Available`, `Bound`, `Released`. The LV kept the pod's group and SELinux label.
- cert-manager v1.21.3 from the OCI chart, with `crds.enabled: true`. A self-signed test certificate got `READY True`.
- TopoLVM 0.42.1, thick LVs, `spare-gb: 0`: `5116Mi` per worker. Its docs say it needs cert-manager. Here it doesn't.
- A test PVC stayed `Pending` until a pod used it, then got a 1 GiB LV on k8s2. The LV went with the PVC.
- The StatefulSet `writer`: two pods, required on different workers, each with its own 1Gi claim and a line every 5 s.
- Patched `data-writer-0` to 2Gi while it ran: `2.0G` in `df` within a second, no gap in the log. The repo keeps 1Gi.
- Check 31 has 40 checks, and `allowVolumeExpansion: false` failed one. After the reboot and the 3 GiB, 25–31 passed.

## What broke
- **k8s1 answered ping but not ssh or port 6443, six and a half minutes after the reboot.** At 2.5 GiB with no swap it thrashed (kube-apiserver had 651 MiB after boot), and its journal stopped before any OOM line. Only `virsh reset` got through.
- **`kubectl exec … static-writer -- id;ls -ln /data` said `ls: cannot access '/data': No such file or directory`.** My shell split the line at `;`, so `ls` ran on the desktop. One `kubectl exec` per command fixed it.
- **`kubectl diff -k` gave `Error from server (NotFound): namespaces "writer" not found`.** The server-side dry run creates nothing, so the namespace is not there for the objects in it. The files were right, and the first `apply -k` was the fix.
- **`kubectl get crd | grep 'cert-manager.io$'` printed nothing, with no error.** Every line ends with the CREATED AT column. `replicaCOunt` in a grep and `-n writed` gave an empty answer the same way.
